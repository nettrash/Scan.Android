Scan 1.10 — passkey QR codes, sharper parsing.

• Recognises passkey sign-in QR codes (FIDO cross-device): shows whether it's for signing in or creating a passkey and when it was made, with a phishing warning. The secret inside is never shown.
• GS1 barcodes with group separators are now read properly, and the batch/lot is no longer dropped.
• Driving-licence barcodes (AAMVA) now show the licence number.
