Scan reads and generates the common 1D and 2D barcodes — and understands what's actually inside the code, not just the raw text.

Point the camera at a Wi-Fi QR and you'll see the network name, security type, and a one-tap shortcut to Wi-Fi Settings. Scan a SEPA invoice and you get the IBAN, beneficiary, and amount as separate copyable rows. Scan a contact card and the system "New Contact" form opens pre-filled. Scan a calendar event and the "Add Event" sheet appears with the title, location, and times in your time zone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Scan never uploads what you scan. Barcodes, printed text and translations are processed on the device by Google's ML Kit — camera frames are analysed in memory and never written to disk — and the history stays in the app's private database. No accounts, no servers of ours, no advertising SDKs. (ML Kit itself sends Google diagnostic statistics, and fetches language models and settings when you translate.)

SAFE, EXPLAINED SCANS

• QR safety check: look-alike addresses, address-bar tricks, risky links, preset payment amounts and stuck-on codes are flagged before you act — a dangerous link offers Copy instead of Open
• "What is this code?" explains any result in plain language, with passwords and secrets blanked out
• Live Text and Bulk modes: read printed text and translate it, or collect a pallet of codes and export CSV
• NFC: read a tag, or write a generated code to one
• Digital-ID, passkey, e-invoice and product-passport codes decoded — Scan never answers an identity request

SMART RESULT SHEET

Scan understands these payload formats and surfaces each field as a tap-to-copy row:

• Web — URL, mailto, tel, sms / smsto
• Wi-Fi — SSID, password, security, hidden flag
• Location — geo: links opened in Maps
• Contact — vCard 3.0 and MECARD
• Calendar — iCalendar VEVENT (UTC, time-zone, all-day)
• Authentication — otpauth:// for authenticator apps
• Retail — EAN / UPC / ITF product codes
• Cryptocurrency — Bitcoin (BIP-21), Ethereum (EIP-681 with chain ID), Litecoin, Bitcoin Cash, Dogecoin, Monero, Cardano, Solana, Lightning (BOLT-11)
• Bank payments — EPC SEPA Payment QR / GiroCode (EU), Swiss QR-bill, Czech SPD, Slovak Pay by Square, EMVCo Merchant QR with drill-down for Pix, PayNow, PromptPay, CoDi, UPI-via-EMVCo, DuitNow, QRIS, FPS, NAPAS, NETS, Indian UPI, Bezahlcode, Serbian NBS IPS QR (Prenesi)
• Mobile-payment apps — Swish, Vipps, MobilePay, Bizum, iDEAL
• Receipts — Serbian SUF fiscal receipt
